About RoomLink

Bridging the gap between students and safe, affordable university housing in West Africa and beyond.

100% On-Ground Inspected

Engineered out of genuine academic struggle.

Every academic year, thousands of eager scholars arriving at regional university campuses like Fourah Bay College, Njala University, and across West Africa encounter the same issues: brutal brokers demanding exploitative upfront commissions, phantom properties, and sub-standard conditions lacking potable water or basic security.

RoomLink was founded directly by former students and technologists tired of watching fellow scholars lose their tuition savings to housing scams. We built a digital sanctuary where property ownership is transparently verified and prices are governed by open standards.

Our Mission & Guiding Story

Redefining the standard of dignity, trust, and predictability for African students seeking their home away from home.

The RoomLink Mission

"To ensure that no university student ever drops out, compromises their personal safety, or incurs predatory financial ruin simply trying to find a decent roof over their head."

OFFICIAL CHARTER MANDATE

Abolishing Shadow Fees

Rigorous 40-Point Audits

Equitable Landlord Alliances
